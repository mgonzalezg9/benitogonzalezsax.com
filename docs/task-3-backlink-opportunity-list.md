# Task 3 Backlink Opportunity List

Last updated: 2026-04-29

This list prioritizes backlink and citation opportunities for Benito González Sax after the profile consistency cleanup.

The goal is not to collect random links.

The goal is to get:

- relevant wedding and event links
- local and regional citations
- partner mentions from real venues and suppliers
- English/international trust signals for destination weddings

## How to use this list

- `Priority` reflects likely SEO / entity value
- `Difficulty` reflects how hard it is to obtain the link
- `Link target` tells us where the link should point
- `Ask type` tells us what kind of mention to request

## Link target rules

- Homepage:
  - use for broad brand links, profiles, and general supplier mentions
- Spanish local pages:
  - use when the source is city- or province-specific
- English homepage or English city pages:
  - use when the source is clearly international or destination-wedding-facing

## Tier 3: Real Partner Backlinks

These are usually the highest-quality links because they come from genuine collaborations.

### 3.1 Venues

Priority: `P1`

Target:
- wedding venues
- fincas
- hotels
- beach clubs
- country houses
- event estates

Link target:
- homepage for broad vendor listings
- city page if the venue is strongly local to Murcia, Alicante, Málaga, Mallorca, etc.

Best ask:
- supplier recommendation page
- “trusted vendors” page
- real wedding recap mentioning live saxophone
- venue blog post about entertainment ideas

Why it matters:
- highly relevant to weddings
- locally grounded
- strong corroboration for Google and LLMs
